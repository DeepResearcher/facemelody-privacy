Privacy policy

Camera permission is requested only for the in-app front/rear camera. Capture and background separation happen on your device.

FaceMelody processes photos only on your device. No account, advertising or analytics service is used. Face landmarks frame the portrait and, in Couple mode, shape musical motifs for both people; no identity or emotion profile is created.

Your selected photo is not sent to a server. The processed image and paths remain in memory during the session. Music, camera photos and share files are temporarily stored in private app cache. Starting a new portrait clears the current session. Share files older than 24 hours are removed on the next export. Camera cache is cleared when the app is opened again. You can remove all local data in Android settings by clearing app storage/cache or uninstalling.

Sharing sends only your selected artwork to the app you choose in the Android share sheet. That app’s privacy rules apply. The bundled demo is an AI-generated portrait of a fictional person.

MP4 video export is available through a one-time Google Play purchase. Google Play processes payment and returns purchase status to the app; FaceMelody does not receive your card details. The app checks your purchase with Google Play when opened or when you restore it. Your portrait and music are not sent to Google Play for this purchase.
